# Memepot Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** May 11, 2026
## 1. Introduction
Memepot ("the Extension") is a browser extension that helps users save, organize, and copy meme images locally. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.
## 2. Data Collection
**We do not collect any personal data or user content.**
All data processed by Memepot is stored **locally on your device** and never transmitted to external servers, the developer, or any third parties.
## 3. What Data Is Stored Locally
The Extension stores the following information **only on your local device**:
- **Images and memes** you choose to save (via right-click "Pot it" or file import)
- **Meme metadata** such as tags, titles, source URLs, and categories
- **Application settings** including theme preference, grid size, and copy format
- **Usage statistics** such as copy count and last-used timestamps (used for local sorting only)
All of the above is stored using:
- **IndexedDB** (via Dexie.js) for images and metadata
- **`chrome.storage.local`** for settings
## 4. Network Requests
The Extension makes network requests **only** to fetch image binary data (Blobs) from URLs you explicitly select via the right-click context menu. These requests:
- Are **initiated by you** (user action only)
- Retrieve **image files only** (PNG, JPEG, WebP)
- Do **not** send any personal data, identifiers, or analytics
- Are **not** used for tracking or profiling
## 5. Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties.
## 6. Data Retention
- Saved memes remain in your local library until **you** delete them.
- Items in the **Inbox** or **Trash** are automatically deleted after **48 hours** to manage local storage.
- You may manually export or delete all data at any time via the Settings page.
## 7. Backup & Export
Memepot allows you to export your library as a `.zip` file to your local downloads folder. This export:
- Is **initiated manually by you**
- Contains **only your locally stored data**
- Is **not uploaded** to any server
## 8. Security
Since all data remains on your local device, security is governed by your browser's sandboxing and storage protections. We do not operate remote servers that could be breached.
## 9. Children's Privacy
Memepot does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The Extension does not collect any data at all.
## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
